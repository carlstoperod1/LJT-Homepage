Hello! I'm Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I'm passionate about natural language processing and machine learning.

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I also had the privilege of being advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring how to enhance the reasoning capabilities of large language models through reinforcement learning techniques.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Investigating the causes and mitigation strategies for hallucination problems in vision-language models.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Studying the fundamental mechanisms behind the truthfulness of LLMs and how to interpret their internal representations.
